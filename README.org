** Concept Overview
:tag:idea:premise:ironic-domesticity:
Spouse Points is a tongue-in-cheek relationship utility for *recognition* in long-term partnerships — especially emotional and domestic labor that usually goes unnamed. It is not a chore app, not Fair Play, not arbitration. Product truth and mechanics live in [[file:SPEC.md][SPEC.md]]; the ship order lives in [[file:BUILD-v2.md][BUILD-v2.md]] and [[file:ROADMAP-12w.md][ROADMAP-12w.md]].

*Numbers:* early versions stay numeric for sync, SMS, and a shippable loop; the deeper read is *recognitions and how long they last*, not “who is ahead.” Treat headline totals as easy scaffolding, not the thesis — see the debate section in [[file:SPEC.md][SPEC.md]]. Quips and categories are curated in [[file:quips.yaml][quips.yaml]] (and the live [[file:index.html][index.html]] prototype).

** Generational Critique
:tag:generational-analysis:cultural-commentary:

*** Millennial Context
Millennials (b. ~1981-1996) are perfectly positioned for ironic detachment *and* desperate emotional sincerity. Raised on participation trophies and traumatized by the 2008 crash, they are:
- Emotionally self-aware but avoidant.
- Technologically fluent, but burnt out by the “app-ification” of everything.
- Obsessed with “adulting,” and unable to maintain systems that feel like work.

Spouse Points fits their need for *low-stakes engagement* and *performative intimacy*. They’ll download it. They’ll mock it. They’ll use it more than they admit.

*** Gen Z Avoidance
Gen Z (b. ~1997-2012) may find the concept cringe and overly structured, preferring vibes over systems. Points imply rules. Rules imply stress. They’re out—unless it becomes a meme.

*** Gen X Indifference
Gen X (b. ~1965-1980) won’t use this. They invented sarcasm and would rather go bowling. Targeting them would be like handing a Tamagotchi to a wolf.

** Execution (current)
:tag:product:strategy:minimalism:

The shipped direction is a **vanilla** single-page app: local state, optional **#state=** URL sync, **sms:** handoff, **PWA** — no backend (see [[file:SPEC.md][SPEC.md]]). As of 2026-04-13, [[file:index.html][index.html]] implements [[file:BUILD-v2.md][BUILD-v2]] through **Step 10** (fixed points, URL sync packet with **A/B orientation swap** when partners’ installs disagree on who is “slot A,” award + request + pending-award SMS handoffs, silent pass, 1×/2×/3× multiplier in award mode only, settings + category CRUD, installable shell). **Device model:** each install is **one partner’s phone** — Partner A is *you* on that device, Partner B is your spouse; you do **not** switch personas in the UI. You award to B, request as A, and text the link to the other person; both still see the two running totals. Older ideas (Notion ledger, bot-of-the-day, pet tied to totals) stay **deprioritized** unless they serve that loop; anything that makes “who has more” the hero fights the mirror.

** Risks & Anti-Features
:tag:warning:satire:emotional-landmines:
- Emotional labor backlash (“I don’t want to gamify my marriage, Susan.”)
- **Numeric gravity:** two running totals invite “winning” under stress unless UI and copy keep centering *noticed*, not *ahead*.
- Scorekeeping spiral leading to genuine resentment.
- **Default politics:** any starter grid carries gender and calendar baggage — mitigated by **obvious editability** and optional seasonal weight, not by pretending defaults are neutral ([[file:SPEC.md][SPEC.md]], *Seasonal labor, default spaces…*).
- **Cross-cultural “packs”** without real co-authors risk hollow or extractive tone — same spec section.
- High potential for people taking a joke app seriously and ending up in therapy.

** North Star Theme
:tag:purpose:soul:
> Appreciation should not require a scoreboard—but maybe a little scoreboard helps us laugh while we learn to notice each other better.

The scoreboard line is the **hook**; the soul is **mirroring**. When in doubt, ship the text-your-partner loop before new surfaces.
